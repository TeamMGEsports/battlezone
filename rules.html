General rules

  1. Entry is free. One registration per team, per match.
  2. Use your real BGMI character ID. Wrong IDs are removed from the room.
  3. No emulators, hacks or teaming with other squads. Breaking this means a ban.
  4. Room ID and password appear on the match page 15 minutes before start.
  5. Join the room on time. Late teams are not waited for.
  6. The captain posts the final result screenshot in the WhatsApp group.
  7. Prizes are paid by UPI within 24 hours of results being posted.
  8. Organizer decisions on disputes are final.

Points

Placement points plus 1 point per kill.
