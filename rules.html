Rules & points

General rules

  1. One registration per team, per match, per phone. Registration closes 30 minutes before start.
  2. Use your real BGMI character IDs. Captains can edit or cancel their team until registration closes.
  3. Room ID and password appear on the match page only for confirmed teams, on the phone used to register, shortly before start. Teammates can see them with “Find my registration”. Never share them outside your team.
  4. Join your own slot number. Players who aren’t on the slot list, or are in the wrong slot, are removed from the room.
  5. No emulators, hacks or teaming with other squads. Breaking this means a ban from all matches.
  6. Join the room on time. Late teams are not waited for.
  7. The captain posts the final result screenshot in the WhatsApp group.
  8. Prizes are paid by UPI within 24 hours of results being posted.
  9. Late entries are only at the organizers’ discretion, and only if slots are free.
  10. Problems with a match or its results: use “Report a problem” on the match page within 1 hour of results. Organizer decisions are final.
  11. Paid matches: if we cancel a match, entry fees are refunded within 24 hours.

Prizes & leaderboard

  • Prizes go to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd place teams and the MVP (the player with the most kills). Each match page shows the amounts.
  • The leaderboard ranks teams by wins, then 2nd places, then 3rd places, then MVPs.
  • Results are taken from the end-of-match screen. Organizer decisions are final.
