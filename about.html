About TeamMG Esports

We run BGMI matches every night for our community: two matches daily, squads and solos, with prizes for the top 3 and the MVP. Most matches are free to enter.

How prizes work

  • Each match page lists the prizes for 1st, 2nd, 3rd and MVP before you register.
  • Prizes are sent by UPI to the winners within 24 hours of results being posted.
  • Once a prize is sent, the results page shows ✓ Paid next to it.

Paid matches and refunds

  • The entry fee is shown on the match page. Your slot is confirmed after we check your UPI payment.
  • If we cancel a match, every entry fee is refunded within 24 hours.
  • If you can’t play after paying, message us before registration closes.

Fair play and disputes

  • Room details only go to registered teams, and the room host removes anyone who isn’t on the slot list.
  • Hackers, emulators and teaming are banned from all matches.
  • Problems with a match or its results: use Report a problem on the match page within 1 hour of results. Organizer decisions are final.

Privacy

To run matches we collect the captain’s WhatsApp number, each player’s in-game name and BGMI ID, and, when needed, a YouTube handle (subscriber matches) or UPI transaction ID (paid matches).

Only organizers can see these details. Everyone else only sees your slot number and team name. We use them to run matches, check entries and pay prizes, and we never sell or share them. To have your details removed, message us.
